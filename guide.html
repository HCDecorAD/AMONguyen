DRESS BY OCCASION

BUSINESS

Opt for Black or Dark Brown Oxfords. Keep detailing minimal.

SMART CASUAL

Derbies or Loafers in rich suede or textured leather. The perfect middle ground.

WEEKEND

Premium Sneakers or relaxed Loafers. Comfort without compromising refinement.

EVENING

Patent leather or highly polished black Oxfords. Absolute formal precision.
